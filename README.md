# Dust Cloaks: early playtest build

Lead a company of mercenaries through the founding of their company, a season of contracts, and the Long Road.

**Play:** https://pjdoranbrown.github.io/dust-cloaks-play/ (a computer browser, Chrome or Firefox; not phones yet). Start a **new game with Lessons on**. Sound on if you can.

**Tell us what you thought:** [First look form](https://github.com/Pjdoranbrown/dust-cloaks-play/issues/new?template=first-look.yml) (needs a free GitHub account). No account? Send your thoughts to Patrick directly.

This is an early build: expect rough edges. Saves stay in your own browser.
